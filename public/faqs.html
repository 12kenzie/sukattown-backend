General

What Is Sukattown?

SukatTown is an IoT system which tracks electric consumption with a digital real-time dashboard that identifies trends or patterns, and makes a prediction for efficient power management.

What is the purpose of SukatTown?

SukatTown aims to help public schools save energy and limit carbon emissions by tracking energy consumption and displaying a real-time dashboard that helps school administrators identify patterns or trends and provide a prediction for cost-effective power management to guide them to formulate actionable steps for management.

How does SukatTown collect energy data?

SukatTown collects energy data through a PZEM-004T module and integrates Various sensors that track real-time electricity usage.
